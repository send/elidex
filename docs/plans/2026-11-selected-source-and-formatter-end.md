# P-A: Selected source and formatter completion

## §1. Producer and consumer contract

Under the [page-source program](2026-11-page-source-cursor.md), Block, inline collection, ordinary whole Flex items and Table rows, Multicol and whole-row Grid describe the definitive selection in a stable owner's source coordinates. Only that owner's selection commit writes its child terminal. Measure/probe, rejected line or avoided break, and positioned side effects cannot write it. A winning empty participating box can finish; excluded descendants contribute no source. Preserve exposed children of contents, distinguish collapsed whitespace from a selected line, and separate first/last in-flow box propagation from source participation. Keep stable IDs across flattened wrappers. Consumers are parent stack/inline owners, P-B's evidence validator and P-C's generation-filtered traversal.

### Positive accounting at definitive selection (PM 1187)

The private selector certifies source exhaustion against the owner's immutable logical input. Formatter ends and emitted boxes are candidate evidence. Neither a supplied Complete nor a tokenless return can define the population that they purport to exhaust. This implements the umbrella §1 owner/item complement inside P-A. P-B still validates overall progress, routes and outstanding geometry; P-C still publishes its run result.

1. **Establish the expected population independently.** Capture the run's composed source, styles, generated text, dispatch content behaviour and stable identities before the first probe or anonymous-box mutation. A new DOM/style revision starts a fresh snapshot and empty coverage. Layout fixup must not rewrite that snapshot. For repeated layout, retain provenance of original source children across reusable anonymous boxes; after an authored edit reconcile original identities against the new snapshot before any pool reuse. If authoritative source or a traversal suffix cannot be established, mark the population open at that boundary. An open population cannot prove exhaustion.
2. **Project by selecting owner.** Enumerate obligations from that snapshot, independently of the formatter's filtered child vector, bounded occupancy table, measured height and emitted fragments. Use the participation distinctions below: transparent contents expose original descendants in order; excluded subtrees and comments have no obligation; positioned anchors use the separate ledger. A participating float, spanner, empty box, caption, row or whole item has a stable terminal obligation. Inline text uses original item/range and its applicable terminal; track suppressed source separately from visible glyphs. A visible anonymous Flex/Grid text sequence keeps its source identity even while dispatch lacks an item. A virtual table row/group/cell maps to the ordered source entities it owns, including orphan cells under contents. Synthetic wrappers do not replace these identities or acquire extra authored terminals. A physical repeated header maps to the original row. Derive nested owner and direct-item keys from the same snapshot; credit to a different owner cannot pay another owner's debt.
3. **Compare at the winner.** For owner O, the selector validates incoming coverage and candidate-local positive additions against O's expected obligations. Only definitive admitted evidence can cover an interval or terminal. Union valid incoming and winning additions; subtract the result from expected source. Reject foreign IDs, invalid bounds or unproven descendants. Probes, an absent child callback and repeated physical membership add nothing. At a hole keep only the proven portion consistent with the selected flow/order and boundary; later siblings in a sequential flow cannot certify a missing predecessor. Independent parallel flows retain only the evidence permitted by their own selection. Persist additions separately from cumulative coverage.
4. **Return the end.** Source Complete requires a closed population, empty complement and no unresolved selected nested end. A legitimate pending route for remaining source gives Continue only if it can resume the correct owner and first outstanding work; a missing, stale or unusable route gives Incomplete with the admitted prefix and no invented continuation. An open population or unresolved nested outcome also prevents Complete. The Whole reader still converts an escaping Continue to Incomplete. Source exhaustion alone cannot erase a P-B geometry or boundary obligation. Empty input legitimately exhausts; a zero-height row or child still owes its own terminal.
5. **Falsify the rule without a known producer bug.** Supply a plausible tokenless Complete and equal box geometry while silently deleting an expected ID, text suffix or empty-item terminal from an otherwise successful producer. Check the exact nonempty complement and denied enclosing terminal even if the omitted path appears nowhere in the audit. Repeat with a duplicate ID and wrong-owner credit; neither fills the hole. Restore only the missing evidence and expect exhaustion. Repeat with a depth-limited population walk: the unknown suffix keeps it open. The twelve audit rows below guide test selection, not certification of completeness.

**Project the formatter's content behaviour.** Before measurement, resolve the dispatch decision for the actual box in the immutable run. Have that same decision supply its descendant policy to both the definitive formatter and the projection. A formatter establishing a formatting context for children projects the ordinary in-flow, nested and positioned obligations. Only a dispatch whose content is established to be external to the CSS formatting model and whose descendant layout is suppressed projects a leaf: its parent owes the selectable item, with no descendant source or anchors. Intrinsic sizing, native appearance, a component or tag alone, a box-only return, and an omitted callback do not establish external content. Where dispatch cannot establish the behaviour, preserve potential descendant obligations and keep the population open. A known faulty suppression on main cannot certify a leaf; gate that producer on Layout receiver #11-button-descendant-layout (PM 1191) until it can supply the required behaviour. Freeze before any probe, renew after a relevant input change. [CSS Display 3, replaced element](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-display-3/#replaced-element) · [HTML §15.5.3 Button layout](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/rendering.html#button-layout) · [§15.5.4 The button element](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/rendering.html#the-button-element-2)

**Private selector falsifiers.** Use a fixed 60×30 flex button with its real button control state in both runs. Give it one in-flow child C. Keep the immutable source, chosen flex dispatch, geometry, and reported Complete identical. Delete only button→C from the selected candidate: the exact outstanding obligation is C's terminal, the result without a usable route is Incomplete, and the button earns no enclosing terminal. Restore that one terminal: the complement is empty and the button may finish. In a sibling with positioned O, verify O's independent anchor without granting it source credit. Keep fully_assigned_replaced_flex_item_skips_its_dom_children: the image-backed Block item with externally supplied content earns its own terminal and neither child nor anchor is owed. Remove ImageData from its twin and verify ordinary child and anchor participation. Across supported image, embedded frame and form-control dispatches, apply the same three-way property: proven external content, descendant-formatting, or open. A single component type may reach different formatting behaviours; test each distinct reachable behaviour and keep unproved combinations open.

**Transform projection.** For each (owner,text item) enumerate the expected half-open intervals in the ordered SourcePosition coordinate (original UTF-8 byte, expansion ordinal). Use the immutable source and the same deterministic collapse/transform mapping as inline collection, independently of which intervals the winning candidate reports. Preserve the next original boundary as the end of the final expansion unit. Compare and subtract in both coordinates, coalescing only actually adjacent ranges for the same key.

Falsifier at the private reader: uppercase “ﬃz” has boundaries a=(0,0), b=(0,1), c=(0,2), d=(3,0), e=(4,0). The three expansion units occupy [a,b), [b,c), [c,d); Z occupies [d,e). For credit [a,b) the complement is [b,e). Supplied Complete without a usable route yields Incomplete and no text-item terminal. With a valid route at b, resume with [b,e) once, preserve [a,b) as incoming and earn the terminal only on exhaustion. For credit [a,b) plus [c,e), the complement must be exactly [b,c), even though the producer visited the original ligature byte and the suffix. Reject a claimed terminal; a valid route at b must skip already credited [c,e). The intact [a,e) control with resolved end finishes once. Supply these intervals to the private selector; keep actual line breaking at its existing supported clusters.
