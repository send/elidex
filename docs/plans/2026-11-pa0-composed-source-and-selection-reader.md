# P-A0 — immutable composed input and the private selecting reader

## §1. Landing boundary

P-A0 is the first provider in the [ratified six-slice amendment](2026-11-selected-source-formatter-slices.md). Its observable positive result is the **real private reader’s** per-owner selection from controlled producer evidence. Its observable dispatch result is **Incomplete for every formatter family**, including closed empty boxes. At this landing, the reader can validate a supplied candidate, but no formatter can produce definitive private evidence. This distinction is essential to testing the base in isolation.

**ECS traversal.** In elidex-ecs, share the existing live shadow-host, assigned-slot and ordinary-child branch decision between composed enumeration and the evidence-returning walk (PM 1194). Retain public order, ordinary/shadow caps, stale-host fallback and the stored assigned vector, including repeated IDs. Preserve the existing uncapped compatibility walk’s missing-relation and internal-shadow-root behavior. The evidence walk returns the verified ordered prefix and the first interrupted position. Validate relation, identity and repetition before admitting each child. The public sibling cap applies to public enumeration. The evidence walk continues through all verified siblings, while Layout records interruptions from its own flatten-depth guard. ECS reports tree facts; Layout interprets display, flattens transparent contents and stops the outer enumeration immediately when a nested walk stops.

**Immutable Layout input.** Capture ordered obligations, text coordinates, dispatch content behavior, separate positioned identities and the stop reason before any probe or fixup. Use the owner/item pair, even when the item came through a transparent wrapper. Closed empty, known prefix with unknown suffix, and unknown order at owner entry are distinct. PM 1246 requires every unretained AfterLayout capture to stop before reading children, even if the current owner is empty, appears unaffected or is restyled. Replay a current-revision snapshot with its own incoming ledger. After a relevant DOM/style/data edit take a new snapshot and fresh coverage; a previous revision’s credit cannot be supplied as current credit. PM 1248 assigns recovery after destructive public fixup to #11-anonymous-table-fixup-source-preservation.

**Snapshot binding at the reader.** Each capture creates a private identity handle. Cloning or retaining the snapshot shares that handle. Recapture after an edit creates a distinct handle even if all entity IDs, lengths and endpoints match. Construct incoming ledgers, winner evidence and supplied routes through the snapshot and retain its handle through page sharing, copies and replay. At the reader boundary check identity before interpreting each operand: foreign incoming contributes no prior credit, a foreign winner contributes no new evidence or terminal, and a foreign route cannot authorize Continue. Compute the remaining obligations from the current snapshot and its admitted evidence. This is local to private selection and requires no public revision counter.

**Content projection.** Freeze the same dispatch decision that determines whether descendants are formatted. Selectable external leaves require both established external content and suppressed CSS descendant layout; an ordinary formatting context owes its in-flow children and separately positioned identities. Unknown behavior retains potential obligations and an open end. Geometry, intrinsic size, a tag or a component alone cannot prove a leaf. Use the image/control/frame paths in the Block helpers and dispatch decision as inputs to one classification. Flex styling on a button can select descendant formatting even with native control state. Keep faulty button suppression open on #11-button-descendant-layout. A0 tests classification against the frozen data; A1/A2 own real producer twins.

**Reader.** Start with the independent expected population, never the reported candidate list. Validate owner, source identity and each original interval endpoint in the frozen (UTF-8 byte, expansion ordinal) domain before union, for incoming and winning evidence alike. Preserve raw boundaries across stored pages and nested evidence copying until validation. Validate incoming coverage, then admit winning evidence in selection order. Compute remaining obligations as expected source minus the union of valid incoming and **actually admitted additions**. A valid report beyond a missing sequential predecessor remains owed. Within an admitted text item, preserve valid disjoint intervals on either side of a hole. Track terminals independently and return the additions, exact remaining obligations and stop. Do not fill interval holes with a terminal or count a positioned anchor as source. A confirmed sequential prefix ends at its first missing predecessor; an interrupted reorderable Flex/Grid population cannot certify even an observed item that might follow an unseen predecessor. A closed owner with no outstanding source and a resolved supplied end can finish. A supplied route yields Continue only when it identifies the same snapshot and owner and the first outstanding item and position, including its expansion ordinal for text. Unresolved work with an unusable route yields Incomplete. The Whole boundary maps even a valid escaping Continue to Incomplete and withholds the enclosing terminal. P-B decides real progress or stall.

**Entry and ownership.** Route a private request through the layout dispatch boundary before size probes, fixup, collection, empty/depth fast paths and all geometry writes. Gate Block, direct/mixed IFC, Flex, Grid, Table and Multicol with zero additions and no usable route; a throwaway probe emits no definitive evidence. Cover inline display aliases and Multicol dispatch priority. The controlled-evidence fixture invokes the same reader that future winners will call; it does not enable a family or install a fallback to ordinary public dispatch. Keep ordinary screen layout, paged layout and both display-list builders on their existing public input. P-A1 introduces the first private geometry writer and its isolation proof. P-C owns public result publication.

## §2. Coupled invariants and falsifiers

Four invariants intersect here: input identity/provenance, population/order, coverage/end, and dispatch isolation. Each pair has a discriminating observation.

| Pair | Counterexample and oracle |
|---|---|
| Identity × population | A/contents(C)/B before fixup gives [A,C,B]; preserve the original source IDs. Without retained provenance after public layout the verified prefix is empty, including A. |
| Identity × coverage | Same item under another owner, another revision, or a duplicated synthetic row never pays the missing owner/item obligation. Replaying the original immutable input yields the same answer. |
| Identity × isolation | Probe or mutate live style/data after capture. The retained reader input is stable; fresh capture uses the new dispatch behavior or stops cold. The disabled entry leaves parentage and style intact. |
| Population × coverage | A,+1 before an interrupted contents chain hiding B,−1: retain the stop and admit no order-dependent identity. A closed empty chain permits A at the controlled reader; real Flex/Grid dispatch stays disabled. |
| Population × isolation | For each family, feed a closed empty owner, an entirely excluded group, and a visible outstanding item through actual private dispatch. All three return Incomplete there with zero additions and no enclosing terminal. At the controlled reader with resolved supplied Complete and no item evidence, closed empty and fully visited/excluded both have complement [] and end Complete. The visible outstanding case has complement [visible], end Incomplete and no enclosing terminal. |
| Coverage × isolation | Supply complete-looking equal geometry with a missing interval or terminal. Only restoring that exact evidence permits the controlled reader to finish; the same reported end cannot enable a disabled formatter or modify a generation. |
