## §1. Compute once and resolve against the painted box

Use the same corner grammar from the stylesheet declaration dispatcher (declaration.rs and declaration/misc.rs) and registered BoxHandler. Route BoxHandler, the built-in elidex-style resolve/box_model writer, computed getter, explicit inherit and css-anim/apply through C1c typed projection and assignment. Keep the pair at declaration, computed and animation endpoint boundaries. Box owns one pair-aware shorthand serializer for the declaration and computed shorthand queries. Gather all four pairs in corner order, compress each axis independently and omit the slash only if the expanded lists are equal. Preserve CSS-wide and pending-substitution dispatch. Adapt pairs at both live transition and keyframe sampling entries. For matching scalar kinds call C1c’s shared binary64 scalar operation once per component, reassemble, apply and reproject. For unsupported mixed endpoints retain the existing discrete selection before/at half progress. C2 owns generic mixed interpolation. The DOM/harness computed corner getter projects the pair even after paint. Layout owns the border-box reference; paint evaluates horizontal against width and vertical against height each time the geometry changes.

Resolve all four pairs before applying the common overlap reduction factor from the minimum side-length-to-adjacent-radii-sum ratio (bounded above by one, ignoring zero sums). Derive inner radii component-wise from the reduced outer radii: subtract the corresponding horizontal and vertical border thickness, flooring each at zero. Carry pairs through walk.rs, paint::compute_inner_radii, background shapes, border rings, overflow/background clips, shadows where they take corner geometry, display_list.rs and vello_backend.rs. Use elliptical paths for fill, ring and clip, including per-side colored borders. A zero axis makes that corner square. Choose the used contour by the consumer’s edge. Use outer, padding and content contours for the corresponding background-clip on color and images; derive the content radii by additionally subtracting the adjacent padding. Clip applicable overflow at its clipping edge and replaced content at its content edge before emitting the image. Bound arcs to the actual edge even when a thick opposite border truncates an ellipse. At used-shape selection check table role and border-collapse: table, inline-table and cells use the rounded contour in separate mode and square edges in collapse mode, retaining computed pairs. For sliced decorations construct outer, padding and applicable content contours in joined coordinates, intersect each with the fragment interval and translate the intersection into fragment coordinates. Omit borders at the break. Clone constructs each contour against its own fragment box. [CSS Backgrounds §§4.2–4.5](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-backgrounds-3/#corner-shaping)

Reuse C1a public geometry dispatch, C1b optional-reference/formatting-context operations and C1c field assignment. For both stylesheet dispatch and registered handlers use G’s nonnegative length-percentage with auto for preferred/minimum size and none for maximum; retain existing supported intrinsic size branches at their property-specific entries.
