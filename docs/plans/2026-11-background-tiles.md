## §1. Retain values until the positioning area and image size exist

Route the stylesheet background shorthand in declaration/misc.rs, longhands, BackgroundHandler::parse and shorthand::parse_background_shorthand through equivalent, fully consuming grammar. Preserve layer boundaries, slash position/size, origin/clip keywords and resets, including color only on the final layer. For supported sizes accept nonnegative length-percentages, auto and cover/contain; positions admit signed offsets and supported edge/keyword forms. T reparses substituted tokens for the receiving property and expands pending shorthands through its longhand set. G owns supported length admission and contextual conversion, N simplifies at computation and evaluates remaining percentages with a supplied basis. Use the source element context for lengths in positions, sizes, stops, hints, radii and centers. Preserve typed layer values through resolve_background_layers, registered computed getters, C1c typed projection/inherit/assignment and existing background-position endpoint transport. Keep declared spelling/var readback separate from computed projection. C2 owns generic mixed interpolation.

Dispatch gradient parsing in gradient.rs by family, sharing list traversal. Linear and radial positions on the gradient line accept length-percentage, conic positions accept angle-percentage, including supported angular math. Preserve the order and type of color stops, omitted positions, double-position stops and separately represented transition hints. Validate at least two color stops, with a hint only between stops and at most one per adjacent pair. Preserve repeating versus non-repeating identity through computed serialization and paint. Reject unknown units and wrong-family dimensions at the function argument. For radial shapes retain circle/ellipse, applicable extent keywords or explicit radii and center; for conic retain start angle and center. [CSS Images 3 §§3.2–3.4](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images-3/#gradients) and [CSS Images 4 §3.3](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images-4/#conic-gradients)

Use the Box layer list, including none images with a meaningful clip. Match lists by image count, cycling shorter longhand lists; keep specified ordering and draw back to front. Apply the bottom layer’s selected clip to color and each image’s own edge contour to that image. Select each supported attachment’s positioning area and background-origin from published geometry, independently of its painting clip. Use one sizing/positioning operation for URL and all gradient render branches. Resolve explicit size percentages against the positioning area; auto/cover/contain use the applicable intrinsic size/ratio rules (gradients have no intrinsic dimensions). Apply repeat-mode sizing adjustments before final placement as required. For each axis resolve position percentages against positioning-area size minus concrete image size, retaining sign when the image is larger. Translate the resulting tile origin once into paint coordinates. Repeat the image over its painting area using that tile. [CSS Backgrounds §§2.4–2.9](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-backgrounds-3/#background-position)

In builder/paint/mod.rs construct the used gradient from the concrete tile, then pass that same geometry and ordered used stops to the display list and Vello. For linear gradients derive line endpoints from angle/direction and tile dimensions before resolving lengths or percentages. For radial gradients resolve the center against the tile axes, compute the ending shape, and use the center-to-right ellipse radius as the 100% stop distance along the virtual gradient ray. For conic gradients use the tile-local center and clockwise angular sweep, with one turn as 100%. In used coordinates apply default endpoints, nondecreasing stop fixup and even spacing of omitted interior stops. Color adjacent intervals using the hint's nonlinear weighting, including coincident-stop and endpoint-hint cases. For repeating gradients use the first-to-last used stop interval as the period and handle the zero-period solid-color case. Keep computed order, units and dependency intact when the tile or positioning area changes. [CSS Images 3 §3.4](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images-3/#color-stop-syntax)

## §2. Crossing checks

| Crossing | Production observation |
|---|---|
| Declaring route × cascade | Through stylesheet and registered shorthand/longhands compare direct and whole-property var winners at computed projection and painted geometry. Valid calc yielding negative size clamps to zero; verify suppression of the zero-size tile. With --n:5, var(--n)px stays invalid and --d:5px succeeds as a complete size. A malformed gradient in a multi-image declaration invalidates the whole value. |
| Tile × reference | Use a non-root content positioning area 200×80 at (10,20), larger padding/border boxes, tile size 50% 50%, no-repeat and position calc(50% + 2px) 25%. The 100×40 tile starts at (62,30); on a horizontal gradient its calc(50% + 2px) stop is 52px from the tile start. With unchanged style and a 300×120 area at the same origin the 150×60 tile starts at (87,35), stop 77px. Check URL and gradient destinations, computed size/position/stop readback before and after resize and color at a hard stop. With a 20px font substitute 1em for the length term: it contributes 20px independently of the resized basis. Include cover, contain, auto and a negative leftover-space control. |
| Center × family | On the 100×40 tile at (62,30), radial and conic centers at calc(25% + 2px) 75% are locally (27,30), hence painted at (89,60). With explicit radial ellipse 40px 10px, calc(50% + 2px) is 22px along its horizontal gradient ray. Check an extent-keyword sibling and signed out-of-tile center. Conic 90deg is 25% of a turn, irrespective of tile size; compare a hard transition on both sides of the ray with from 0deg and with a rotated start. A deg stop in a linear/radial gradient, a px stop in a conic gradient and an unknown unit reject the declaration. Check direct rejection after a prior URL and winning invalid substitution to none. |
| Hints × actual color | With opaque black/white endpoints at 0/100% and hint at 25%, observe half the blend at the hint and a quarter blend at 6.25% (weight is square root of normalized position). Use a fixed supported color space, compare channel weights or matching reference pixels. Contrast omitted hint, misplaced adjacent hints, omitted interior stops and two-position hard stops. Run linear, radial and angular cases through ordinary and repeating family dispatch; measure a repeated period and its corresponding pixel one period later. Distinguish gradient repetition inside the tile from background-repeat outside it. |
| Layer list × clipping | Retain the Box no-image color fixtures with omitted background-image and explicit none: content-box clip survives computed readback and cuts off color in padding. Use an opaque color and a transparent image/gradient above a none bottom layer, with different clip and origin keywords. Observe bottom color through transparent pixels, top image in its own larger clip, and separate per-layer destinations on a two-image variant. Repeat after shorthand reset, explicit inherit and projection/assignment onto a differently sized target. Re-resolve only used geometry. |
